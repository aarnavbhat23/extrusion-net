# Transfer to asynchronous transport: frozen development protocol

The general memory-architecture screen is stopped. Project direction: learn and
falsify transport mechanisms under independently specified dynamics. This is a
mechanism-learning investigation, not a replacement for attention. Project
selection follows the failed moment screen and a new prior-art check.

Compare synchronized shared removal, asynchronous equal-rate Poisson arms with
shared exponential removal, and independent marginal transport. Gate successes
are time-independent, independent across sites; no motor interactions, retries
or occupied-site exclusion. Source patterns are known. Check continuous-time
reach probabilities using a separate quadrature calculation and an event-count
sampler. Neither is measured biology or an external laboratory simulator.

Derive whether bands at distances two and three distinguish synchronization
from asynchronous timing after gate reparameterization. If not, exhibit the
exact matched family and test the first extra informative band (distance four).
Freeze sizes 16/64, three gate seeds, marginal survivals .5/.8/.95, both timing
families, noise SD 0/.0001/.001/.01. Train/calibrate on two alternating source
patterns. Fit from bands two/three only; use distance-four data to select between
the two fitted families by squared residual. Selection data are not held out.
Test untouched uniform loading, one left cut, and two directional cuts. Report
all family errors, selection accuracy, held-out RMSE, extra observation count
and high-noise failures. Do not claim noisy confidence coverage for point fits.
The static comparator gets exact effective marginal gates (oracle advantage).

Continuation rule: exact indistinguishability proof plus an informative extra
band, independent numerical validation of the continuous-time kernel, and at
least a twofold mean held-out RMSE improvement over forcing the synchronized
family on asynchronous data at SD .001. Selection must be >=90% correct over
both timing families at that noise. A pass only supports the next robustness
study: unequal speeds, non-exponential removal and independently measured data.
It does not establish new molecular physics or a generally superior learner.
