# Four-band recovery of unequal clock speeds: theory-driven follow-up

Added after the equal-speed development screen passed; freeze before testing
unequal-speed outcomes. This is a separate exploratory follow-up, not independent
confirmation of the prior screen. Derive the distance-four directional
coefficients and test whether they identify two step/removal rate ratios, beyond
an equal-speed assumption. Absolute time remains unidentifiable from reach alone.

Sizes 16/64/128, seeds 0/1/2 generated separately from the earlier study, left rate
one, right rates .5/1/2, removal rates .1/.5/1. Uniform gates .2..8. Known two
alternating loadings and contact bands at distances 2/3/4. Noise SD 0/.0001/.001.
Pool band equations by least squares; do not select parameters using held-out
outcomes. Report invalid/physical-boundary projections, relative rate-ratio error,
held-out uniform/left-cut/two-cut message RMSE and an equal-speed point-inversion
control. Preserve weak-rate and noise failures. No noisy coverage claim.

Continue only if noiseless inversion matches exact ratios and gates to 1e-9,
and mean message RMSE at SD .001 is at least twofold smaller than the forced
equal-speed inversion on the unequal-speed cases. This supports further
falsification, not measured biological validity or a new general architecture.
